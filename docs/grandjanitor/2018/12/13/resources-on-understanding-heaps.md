# Resources on Understanding Heaps

Some assorted links for understanding heaps in user-land, 
- All post relevant to strace by [Julian Evans](https://jvns.ca/categories/strace/), and her[strace zine](https://jvns.ca/blog/2015/04/14/strace-zine/), ltrace is pretty doped too.
- Here is a list of [Linux syscalls](http://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/syscalls.2.html)
- Learning what /proc is, check out dmesg is nice too,
- So you can attach gdb to a process, you know that right?
- [A very useful tutorial to follow from Holberton School](https://blog.holbertonschool.com/hack-the-virtual-memory-malloc-the-heap-the-program-break/),
- [Dan Luu's post](https://danluu.com/malloc-tutorial/) on how to write your own malloc
- [A very short book to understand how glibc malloc works](https://heap-exploitation.dhavalkapil.com/introduction.html)
- [Several examples](https://github.com/shellphish/how2heap) of playing with glibc malloc, of course many of these exploits are outdated).
- If you are debugging, you love [valgrind](http://www.valgrind.org/) and its --leak-check function.

# Resources on Speech Recognition

Unlike other deep learning topics, there are no readily made video courses available on speech recognition. So here is a list of other resources that you may find useful.

Books: 
- Rabiner's [Fundamental of Speech Recognition](https://www.amazon.com/Fundamentals-Speech-Recognition-Lawrence-Rabiner/dp/0130151572/ref=sr_1_fkmrnull_1?keywords=Rabiner+speech+recognition&qid=1550504128&s=gateway&sr=8-1-fkmrnull)
- XD Huang et al's [Spoken Language Processing](https://www.amazon.com/Spoken-Language-Processing-Algorithm-Development/dp/0130226165/ref=sr_1_1?keywords=spoken+language+processing&qid=1550504177&s=gateway&sr=8-1)
- [Automatic Speech Recognition: A Deep Learning Approach ](https://www.amazon.com/Spoken-Language-Processing-Algorithm-Development/dp/0130226165/ref=sr_1_1?keywords=spoken+language+processing&qid=1550504177&s=gateway&sr=8-1)by Dong Yu and Li Deng.

If you want to learn from online resources: 
- [Sphinx's Speech Recognition Concepts](https://cmusphinx.github.io/wiki/tutorialconcepts/)
- [Prof. Bhiksha Raj's lectures](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~bhiksha/courses/11-756.asr/spring2013/)
- NYU's [Eugene Weinstein](http://www.cs.nyu.edu/~eugenew)'s [lectures](https://cs.nyu.edu/~eugenew/asr13/).
- [Edinburg's ASR lecture 2019 ](http://www.inf.ed.ac.uk/teaching/courses/asr/lectures-2019.html)(ongoing),
- [CMU Alan Black's lecture on Speech Processing](http://www.speech.cs.cmu.edu/15-492/)

Useful E2E Speech Recognition Lecture 
- [By Adam Coates.](http://videolectures.net/deeplearning2015_coates_speech_recognition/)

Important papers: 
- Deep Neural Networks for Acoustic Modeling in Speech Recognition" by G. Hinton et al
- Supervised Sequence Labelling with Recurrent Neural Networks by Alex Graves

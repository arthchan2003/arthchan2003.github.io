# List of Neuroscience MOOCs

I always have side interest in neuroscience. So here's just a note to myself, I am taking Coursera's Computational Neuroscience for fun. This is the second time I loop through the course with more understanding on neural networks, and it feels like a completely different class. The others are just my casual interest - one day I will go through them one by one.

Just a disclaimer: unlike my Top-5 List in deep learning, I only have amateurish understanding of computer neuroscience. I also only cursory experience of each of these classes. This might change when I finish a significant portion of them (definition: 50%+). But for now, *caveat emptor*!

Computational Neuroscience: 
- UW's [Computational Neuroscience ](https://www.coursera.org/learn/computational-neuroscience)(See my impression [here](/grandjanitor/2017/06/13/impression-of-rao-and-fairhalls-computational-neuroscience/).)
- [Synapses Neuron and Brain ](https://www.coursera.org/learn/synapses)from Coursera (See my impression [here](/grandjanitor/2017/10/07/a-quick-impression-on-the-course-synapses-neurons-and-brains/).)
- [edX's ](http://lcn.epfl.ch/~gerstner/VideoLecturesGerstner.html#partI)[Neuronal Dynamics](https://courses.edx.org/courses/EPFLx/BIO465.1x/4T2014/info), the harder version used by grad students: [Neural Network and Biological Modeling](http://lcn.epfl.ch/~gerstner/VideoLecturesGerstner.html#partI)
- Harvard's [Fundamental of Neuroscience](https://www.mcb80x.org/)
- [EdX's Cellular Mechanism of the Brain](https://www.edx.org/course/cellular-mechanisms-brain-function-epflx-brainx-1)

Neurobiology: 
- Coursera's [Understanding Neurobiology](https://www.coursera.org/learn/neurobiology)
- [Neuronal Basis of Consciousness](http://www.openculture.com/the-neuronal-basis-of-consciousness-course-a-free-online-course-from-caltech) from Caltech
- PU's [Advanced Neurobiology I](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advanced-neurobiology1)
- PU's [Advanced Neurobiology II](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advancedneurobiologyii/)
- HBP's [Neurobiology Lectures](https://education.humanbrainproject.eu/web/neurobiology-for-non-specialists)

Perception: 
- Coursera's [Brain and Space](https://www.coursera.org/learn/human-brain)
- Coursera's [Visual Perception and the Brain](https://www.coursera.org/learn/visual-perception/home/welcome)

Measurement and BCI 
- Coursera's [Functional fMRI, ](https://www.coursera.org/learn/functional-mri/home/welcome)
- [Introduction_To_Modern_Brain-Computer_Interface_Design](https://sccn.ucsd.edu/wiki/Introduction_To_Modern_Brain-Computer_Interface_Design),
- Neurohacking in R

Medical Neuroscience/Neruology 
- Duke's [Medical Neuroscience](https://www.coursera.org/learn/medical-neuroscience/home/welcome)
- Human Brain Project's Courses on [Brain Medicine](https://education.humanbrainproject.eu/web/brain-medicine-for-non-specialists/home)
- Coursera's[Drug and Brain ](https://www.coursera.org/learn/drugs-and-brain)
- [Schizophrenia](https://www.coursera.org/learn/schizophrenia)
- [Introduction to Clinical Neurology](https://www.coursera.org/learn/clinical-neurology)

Behavioral Neuroscience 
- UCLA's [Behavoral Neuroscience Lab](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B40JGsMe2Yo&list=PL881C1F27782297D6)
- [NYU's Brain and Behavior](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLtXCbh6IFA7QCsei-t8WesusKi8I2LXUJ).
- Stanford's [Robert Sapolsky's Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NNnIGh9g6fA&list=PLD7E21BF91F3F9683)

Other Biophysics/Biomedical Engineering-Related 
- Coursera's Bioelectricity classes on The [Mechanism](https://www.coursera.org/learn/extracellular-potentials/home/welcome) and [Quantitative Approach](https://www.coursera.org/learn/bioelectricity/home/welcome)

Cognitive Neuroscience 
- Coursera's [Philosophy to Cognitive Science](https://www.coursera.org/learn/philosophy-cognitive-sciences)
- MIT OCW' [Cognitive Neuroscience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2sSPSVx2f0&list=PLwwc_gbg2bKI_bDJ-cLtvK8h2eq5mE3ZS)

Uncategorized: 
1. Coursera's [Neuroeconomics](https://www.coursera.org/learn/neuroeconomics)
2. Coursera's [Neuromarketing](https://www.coursera.org/learn/neuromarketing)

Other Interesting Sources of Information: [OpenCulture](http://www.openculture.com/biology_free_courses).

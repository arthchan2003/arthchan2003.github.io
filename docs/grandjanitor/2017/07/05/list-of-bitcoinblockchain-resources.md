# List of Bitcoin/Blockchain Resources

As AIDL grew, once in a while people would talk about blockchain would affect AI or deep learning. Currently it is still a long shot, but blockchain by itself is a very interesting technology and it deserves our notice.

Here are some resources you may use to learn about blockchain. Unlike "[Top 5 List](/grandjanitor/2016/08/15/learning-deep-learning-my-top-five-resource/)" for AIDL, I don't really understand the technology too well. But also unlike "[List of Neuroscience MOOC](/grandjanitor/2017/04/10/list-of-neuroscience-mooc/)", [Greg Dubela ](https://www.facebook.com/greg.dubela)did give me a lot of recommendations on what you should learned up. Thus this post is also used as a resource post in "[Blockchain and Crypto](https://www.facebook.com/groups/blockchainnation/)".

Introductory Videos: 
- (2 minute) This [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k53LUZxUF50): explaining the purpose of blockchain in 2 minutes, and the promise it makes.
- [This 6-part series from Dash School ](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLiFMZOlhgsYKKOUOVjQjESCXfR1cCYCod)is a great introductory series on what Blockchain is, how it is governed, and several fundamental concepts. Greg highly recommend the series.

MOOC

Blockchain is still a new development, so it's harder to find MOOC which can teach you the whole thing in entirety. We found there are couple of exceptions: 
- Coursera's [Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency](https://www.coursera.org/learn/cryptocurrency) from Princeton. (See [my quick impression](/grandjanitor/2017/12/20/quick-impression-of-princetons-coursera-bitcoin-and-cryptocurrency-technologies/).)
- [Stanford CS251's Bitcoin class](https://crypto.stanford.edu/cs251/). While this is not a MOOC, you can access all the notes and homework. They are high-quality.
- Coursera's [Cryptography I ](https://www.coursera.org/learn/crypto)and [Cryptography II](https://www.coursera.org/learn/crypto2), which provides you great basics of Cryptography.
- [(HyperLedger-based) Edx Blockchain course](https://www.edx.org/course/blockchain-business-introduction-linuxfoundationx-lfs171x?gclid=EAIaIQobChMI_r_w6pzV1wIVBrbACh0OxwICEAAYASAAEgIfePD_BwE)
- [IBM Blockchain Foundation For Developers](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ibm-blockchain-essentials-for-developers/) - As short course, mostly a class on how to identify and convert a business network to a blockchain-based solution.
- [University of Nicosia's has and Introductory MOOC on bitcoin](https://digitalcurrency.unic.ac.cy/free-introductory-mooc/)., which requires registration. You can also check the their Youtube feed for their [live discussion](https://www.youtube.com/user/MScDigitalCurrency/feed).

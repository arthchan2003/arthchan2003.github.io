For the last two years I have been busy and haven't written anything for a
while.  I was quite busy working as an AI advisor which culminates in me
joining one of my advisees - Stochastic.ai.  I am so glad to work with so
many brilliant and energetic engineers again.

If you are reading this page, you probably know about my background and
care about speech science.  All of our lives have changed since
large-language models have permeated our daily lives.  But what
specifically changes a speech engineer/scientist's life?

## 1. With agents, the pace of work is much faster.

In the past, preparing/running an experiment would take you a long time,
perhaps a few days even when we are talking about small evaluation, SFT,
RL experiments.  With agents I almost always finish them in less than a
day.

## 2. With more work done, you have more time to think about how development can get better.

In the past, after you finish all your experiments, you might only have
1-2 days left to finish your analysis and reporting.  Now you are
constantly in the research phase.  Keeping track of experiments
systematically becomes a tough problem.

Also you are the one who is responsible for the models/systems, so
building a good mental map of what actually happened is important.  In the
past, this is usually a skill set for team, group or division manager; now
you are in charge.  Agents started in different nodes would think slightly
differently, given how the environment seeds them.  Of course, much like
human experimentalists, agents make a lot of mistakes.

## 3. You let agents auto-research

In the past, you let a capable direct report of yours take care of a
project themselves.  Now you let an agent auto-research to generate
surprising insights and explore for your research.

## 4. You still want to listen to the speech

You haven't done it for a long time, haven't you?   As a physical skill,
listening and making observations about the speech and asking how models
would behave accordingly is important.  Technology has advanced, but not
all problems in speech are solved.  You will only know why things go wrong
when you use a speech system and listen to the sample waveforms to
understand why things are working/not working.

## 5. You want to read literature every day

You can certainly use agents to read them (really ... summarize
them). But your understanding of a topic is still the most important
factor of your success.  As a professor once told me (and I rephrase) -
"If you can't read, you can't survive in academia."

## 6. Humans feel smarter ... until you look carefully at what they say/do

Most of your friends/colleagues will be using agents too.  Because of the
agents, they will know a topic much quicker.  But deep understanding can't
be faked with a few prompts to agents.  Agents are language models.  You
can think they are reflecting your thoughts with a special lens.  Agents
amplify human follies and weaknesses in thinking.

## 7. The flip side.

Of course, agents also amplify someone's strengths too! If a person is
logical, cautious, and circumspect about making conclusions, the agent
would follow by setting guardrails during the process.

In other words, agents bring out the best and the worst of us.  It's a
cliche, but it's true.
